Both sites are up.

**Silent Partner** (for any small business)
https://silentpartnerhq.netlify.app

**Silent Partner — the Scot Shop**
https://scotshopsilentpartner.netlify.app

The word for the front door on both is **grassmere**. After that everyone makes their own
account with their own email and password, and everyone on the same site sees the same books
from any device. If someone forgets their password there is a "Forgot your password?" link
that emails them a reset.

Every sign-in and every change is listed under **History**, with a name and a time against it.
Nobody can edit or delete that, which is the point of it.

Both sites open completely empty. Every figure in them will be one somebody typed in.
